Data model

Two entities. Client-generated UUID keys and updated_at on every row from day one, and soft deletes everywhere — all three are free now and expensive to retrofit when sync arrives.

items

FieldType
idUUID, client-generated
titletext — the entire visible content
label_idUUID? — null means Inbox
stateactive | completed
created_attimestamp — also the sort key
updated_attimestamp
completed_attimestamp?
deleted_attimestamp? — tombstone

labels

FieldType
idUUID, client-generated
nametext, without the @ — unique
color_indexint into a fixed palette
created_attimestamp
updated_attimestamp
last_used_attimestamp — drives section order
deleted_attimestamp? — tombstone
