How it works

The overlay stays open

Submit an item and the sheet stays up, the keyboard stays raised, and the label you picked stays selected. Six items to @costco costs six lines of typing and one tag. Tap outside to leave.

One line, one item

Newline is the only separator. Paste six lines, get six items in the order you gave them. “carrots, 2lb organic bag” stays a single item, because that is what you typed — no parsing, no preview, no rules to remember.

Check off without thinking

One tap. No confirmation. The row leaves the list and a four-second undo sits at the bottom of the screen, so a mis-tap costs nothing and never sends you to another screen.

Labels you never create

Type @farmers and the label exists, with a colour picked for you. The store you are actively adding to floats to the top of the list. There is no “new label” button anywhere in the app.

Capture anywhere

Add milk without opening the app

A Quick Settings tile drops the Quick Add overlay straight into the notification shade. It is the same component as the in-app button — same sticky label, same stay-open submit — and it works from the lock screen without opening the app.
